About the business

Built to do the work right.

Next Level Property Solutions is an owner-operated Rhodelia business focused on practical equipment work, straightforward estimates and finished results that hold up.

Steven Swift · Owner

The person who quotes it stays close to it.

Steven built Next Level around the kind of work a good skid-steer and excavator can transform: rough yards, overgrown ground, washed drives, poor drainage and sites that need to be ready for what comes next.

The business serves homeowners, lake-property owners, farms and small commercial clients. The goal is not to be the cheapest name on every estimate. It is to understand the job, price the real scope and leave the property in a condition both sides can be proud of.

Steven handles the conversations directly, visits the site when an estimate cannot be responsibly given over the phone and sends a professional estimate before work begins. That owner-level communication is part of the service—not an upgrade.

What the name stands for

Simple principles. Better jobs.

Property work can get complicated quickly. These are the standards that keep the process clear.

  • 01
    Show up preparedAsk the right questions, look at access and drainage, and understand the site before promising a price.
  • 02
    Be honest about the scopeIf the project needs more than a quick pass—or if it is not the right fit—say that before equipment arrives.
  • 03
    Stand behind the resultGood work includes accountability. If something needs attention, the conversation does not end with the invoice.
  • 04
    Keep improvingInvest in dependable equipment, professional estimates and consistent communication as the company grows.
